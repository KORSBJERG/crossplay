Open word lists

Suggestions from these lists can differ from words accepted in the games. Names, inflections, and rare words may be missing or included differently.

  • English: public-domain ENABLE list, compiled by M. Cooper and A. Beale. Source.
  • Danish: Stavekontrolden, copyright Foreningen for frit tilgængelige sprogværktøjer. Based on DSL data. MPL 1.1 selected from its tri-licence. Notice · Original .dic source · Original .aff source. Our transformation includes direct lowercase words and one-step inflections; unrestricted compounds are not generated.
  • Swedish: OpenSLR SLR29 pronunciation lexicon (Nordic Language Technology / National Library of Norway, updated by Emelie Kullmann), CC0. Pronunciations removed, word forms retained. This corpus list is not SAOL and may include names. Source & notice.
  • Norwegian Bokmål: Norsk Ordbank, Språkrådet / University of Bergen, distributed by Språkbanken at the National Library of Norway. CC BY 4.0. List extraction by Ondkloss. Source & notice.
  • Dutch: approved OpenTaal base words, copyright Stichting OpenTaal and named contributors. Revised BSD selected from the dual licence. Full notice.
  • German: German word list for word games by Enz, CC0. ß is normalized to SS for tiles. Source & notice.

All lists are filtered to their game's tile alphabet, 2–15 tiles, uppercased and deduplicated. Builds pin upstream versions and verify downloaded word-list hashes. Transformations are available in the source repository.
