Your games stay on your device.

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

Next Move reads screenshots, searches word lists, and looks up bundled definitions on your device. It does not upload screenshot pixels, board contents, rack letters, or selected words to an AI provider or dictionary service.

Saved information

Game boards, letters, settings, and a separate session for each game and language are saved in this browser's local storage. Screenshots themselves remain in memory only. Custom word lists are used for the current session. No account, advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK is used.

Web and native versions

The web version downloads application files, dictionaries, and recognition models from its hosting provider. The provider may retain ordinary access logs such as IP address and time of access. The native iOS configuration bundles these application assets locally; it does not point at a remotely hosted website.

External source/licence links open only when selected. Those external sites have their own privacy policies.

Your controls

Clear an individual board using Clear board. To remove all saved game sessions and settings in the web version, clear this site's data in your browser. In a native build, uninstalling the app removes its local app data.

Contact: PEAKNET. If the app's publisher or data practices change, this notice must be updated before release.
